01 / A little about me

Reliable IT starts
with listening.

My IT journey began in 2016 after earning my Computer Systems Servicing NC II certification.

While supporting my college education, I provided IT services to local internet cafés—installing Windows and applications, setting up computer hardware and server-client systems, and handling structured cabling.

I began my professional career as a Technical Engineer at Northgate Technologies, repairing computers and installing operating systems, software, and CCTV systems. I then joined Support Services Group as a Service Desk Support professional, helping stores worldwide with IT infrastructure, hardware, connectivity, and VR systems.

Today, I’m a Network and System Administrator at Phil National Lines, where I support company networks and business systems. My work includes upgrading network infrastructure, setting up Windows Server, Active Directory, and NAS storage, and helping departments prepare for ERP implementation.
